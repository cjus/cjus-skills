## In short

The first chapter of the reports fixture covers two lines the checker reports without failing:
a sentence past the stop, and a callout holding more than one idea.

[1-1] A report is a line the checker prints when it finds something worth reading that no rule
fails on. The obvious move is to treat every report as a failure, and that would fail books
that are correct.

## A sentence past the stop

[1-2] This paragraph holds one sentence that runs well past the stop the spec sets for a
sentence, because it keeps adding clauses after its point has landed, first about the checker,
then about the reader, then about the book, and finally about nothing in particular at all.
Short sentences follow it.
