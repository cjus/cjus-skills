## In short

The third chapter of the reports fixture covers a phrase that recurs across chapters, which is
the mark a template leaves.

[3-1] A stock phrase used in one chapter is invisible. The obvious move is to reach for it again
in the next chapter, and by the third it reads as a template.
<!-- src: fill -->

## Where it shows

[3-2] The same few words open a passage in each chapter, and a reader who jumps between chapters
hears them as a formula.
<!-- src: fill -->

## What the checker does

[3-3] It counts four-word runs once per chapter and reports any that appear in three or more.
