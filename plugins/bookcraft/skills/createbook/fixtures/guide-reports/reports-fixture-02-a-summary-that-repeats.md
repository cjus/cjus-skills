## In short

The second chapter of the reports fixture covers a summary that runs long and repeats its own
chapter. A summary written by copying is the easiest one to produce, and the reader pays for it:
they read the same sentence twice within a minute. Every summary here is meant to be short enough
to read at a glance, which this one is not, because it keeps going after it has said what the
chapter settles, and then it adds a second paragraph that restates the first in other words, and
then it says it again. The opening paragraph already says what the chapter is about, so lifting
it feels like saving work, and this sentence exists only to push the count past the stop.

[2-1] A copied summary costs the reader time because they meet the same sentence twice within a
minute. The obvious move is to copy the opening paragraph, and that is the move to avoid.
