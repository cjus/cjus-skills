# Glossary

**carried-in line** (ch. 1) The first paragraph of an overview section, naming the
concepts a chapter reintroduces from earlier ones.

**walk** (ch. 1) The part of an overview section that says what a chapter argues,
in the order it argues it.

**ceiling** (ch. 2) A limit that a value may not exceed and is under no pressure
to reach.
