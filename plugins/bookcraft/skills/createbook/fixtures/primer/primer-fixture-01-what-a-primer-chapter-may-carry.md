## No header table

A primer chapter opens straight on its subject. The rows a guide chapter carries above its prose, what it draws on and what it fills in, move to the closing section, where a reader who wants them can find them.

<!-- src: fill -->

## Any number of sections

The guide and narration profiles hold a chapter to two or three parts, because each part is one move in an argument. A primer chapter is an explanation, and an explanation of three ideas with a worked example may want five sections or one.

<!-- src: fill -->

### A subsection

An H3 inside a section is allowed, as it is under the guide profile. An H4 is not.

<!-- src: fill -->

## Paragraph length

Most paragraphs here are short. Under the primer profile the paragraph limit is a ceiling the prose rarely reaches, so this next paragraph, which runs past it on purpose, is reported and does not fail the run. It goes on a little longer than it needs to, describing the rule it tests: a paragraph should follow its thought, and a thought occasionally takes more room than usual. The checker names the paragraph so a person can decide whether it earns the room. Nothing else about this chapter would be flagged, since every unit carries its mark and the closing section is last.
