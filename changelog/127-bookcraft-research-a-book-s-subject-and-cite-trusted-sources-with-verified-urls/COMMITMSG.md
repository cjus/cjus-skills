bookcraft: research a primer's subject and cite trusted sources with verified URLs

research-ledger.sh is the only definition of research/ledger.json: check validates the format and that every URL is under a trusted domain, verify fetches each page (or reads an operator-saved copy) and finds each quote, list prints it, and --file serves a research agent's own ledger.
The trusted check parses the URL, refusing look-alikes by suffix, query, fragment, user name, backslash and ".." segments, and verify fails a redirect off the list.
check-provenance.sh resolves web <id> marks, reserves the word, and fails a cited entry a chapter never links or a URL in its prose the ledger lacks; code is not read.
primer.md and chapter-prose.md set the order of authority and the citation rules, and allow links to ledger pages.
createbook/SKILL.md adds the research stage and rewrites the URL rules, the gate, prompt item 8 and step 7; updatebook/SKILL.md requires a verified entry before a new outside fact is cited.
fixtures/research covers the marks and links through manifest rows, and the ledger tool offline through run.sh.
